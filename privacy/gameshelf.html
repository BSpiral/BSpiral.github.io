Game Shelf Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 4, 2026

Game Shelf does not require user accounts, login, or account creation.

Game Shelf does not use analytics and does not collect usage or behavioral analytics.

Advertising

Dot Commander may display advertising provided by third-party advertising services. These services may process information necessary to provide and operate advertisements in accordance with their own privacy policies.

Purchases

Game Shelf offers optional purchases through Google Play, including removal of advertising and Game Packs.

Payment processing is handled by Google Play. Game Shelf does not receive or store payment card or banking information.

Game Shelf may receive purchase-status information from Google Play as necessary to verify purchases and provide purchased content or features.

Game and App Data

Game Shelf does not provide user accounts or cloud synchronization. Game progress, settings, and other locally generated Game Shelf data are stored on the user's device.

Game Shelf itself does not provide general internet or online gameplay functionality. Third-party services used for advertising and Google Play purchases may communicate over the internet as necessary to provide those services.
