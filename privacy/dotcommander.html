Dot Commander Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 4, 2026

Dot Commander does not require user accounts, login, or account creation.

Dot Commander does not use analytics and does not collect usage or behavioral analytics.

Advertising

Dot Commander may display advertising provided by third-party advertising services. These services may process information necessary to provide and operate advertisements in accordance with their own privacy policies.

Game and App Data

Dot Commander does not provide user accounts or cloud synchronization. Game progress, settings, and other locally generated Dot Commander data are stored on the user's device.
